## 🎯 Phase 1: Initial Outreach (Establishing Partnerships)

**The Introductory Partnership Model:**
Brands appreciate demonstrable value before committing to long-term advertising budgets.
- **The Strategy:** Reach out to local sports bars, independent merch brands, or e-commerce sites. The approach: *"We would love to feature your brand in our 'Prize Draw Room' for one week at no upfront cost. If you can provide a $25 or $50 gift card as the prize, we will place your logo front and center for our highly engaged sports audience."*
- **Why it works:** It provides immediate, tangible value to our users while allowing the sponsor to see the platform's engagement rates firsthand. Once the value is proven, we can transition to a standard sponsorship model.

**Hyper-Niche Alignment:**
Because we have separated rooms (Soccer and NHL), we can pitch highly targeted sponsors. Advertisers pay a premium for targeted audiences.
- **NHL Room:** Hockey stick brands, skate sharpening companies, local rinks.
- **Soccer Room:** Pubs that show early morning Premier League games, local soccer leagues, jersey shops.

## 🚀 Phase 2: Future "B2B Features" Roadmap (Scaling Revenue)

*As our user base grows, we will build these technical features into the Arena to justify charging premium rates.*

### 1. The Sponsor Analytics Dashboard 📊
Brands want data to prove ROI. 
- **Feature:** Build a `Sponsor Portal` where they can log in and see:
  - Total views of their logo/prize.
  - Click-through rate (CTR) to their website.
  - Total entries into their specific Draw Room event.

### 2. Branded Gamification Cosmetics 👕
We have a robust cosmetic system. A sponsor can pay to put a **"Branded Frame"** or **"Branded Avatar"** in the shop.
- **Feature:** "The Red Bull Frame". It costs 0 Tokens. When users equip it, they become walking billboards for the sponsor on the Leaderboards.

### 3. Sponsored "Daily Login" Rewards 🎁
The system gives out free Tokens and Tickets daily. We can inject a sponsor here for maximum visibility.
- **Feature:** A popup reading: *"Today's daily login bonus is sponsored by [Brand Name]. Here is a 10% discount code to their store, plus your 25 Arena Tokens!"*

### 4. Custom Sponsored Polls 🙋‍♂️
We have a custom prediction engine. A sponsor can pay to host a specific, highly visible poll.
- **Feature:** *"Who will score the first goal tonight? Pick correctly to win an exclusive Ticket to the [Sponsor] Draw Room."*
